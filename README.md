## The Problem
Distractions are everywhere. Your phone constantly pulls your attention away, breaking your flow and ruining productivity.

## The Solution: Zenple
Lock your phone, beat distractions, and boost productivity with a focus timer. Zenple helps you stay in the zone by turning your work sessions into focused, productive blocks of time.

### Why Zenple?
- ⏱️ **Focus Timers:** Choose 25, 50, 90 minutes or set your own custom session to lock in your focus.
- 📊 **Category Insights:** Tag sessions (Learning, Fitness, etc.) and view trend charts to track your productivity journey.
- 🎯 **Tokens & Goals:** Collect small tokens and stay motivated with rotating slogans to keep your productivity high.
- 🔕 **Beat Distractions:** Subtle chimes and reminders you can tune to maintain absolute focus.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First:** Your data stays private by design, so you can focus on what matters. 

## Use Cases

### For Students
```markdown
1. Open Zenple and set a 50-minute timer for the "Learning" category.
2. Lock your phone to beat distractions.
3. Take a short break when the chime rings to recharge.
4. Review your weekly study trends to optimize your learning.
```

### For Engineers & Knowledge Workers
```markdown
1. Set a 90-minute deep-work session in Zenple.
2. Put your phone away and lock it into focus mode.
3. Boost your productivity and track your time to understand your daily workflow.
```
